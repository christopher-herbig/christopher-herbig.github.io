Preprints and Publications

My research is primarily concerned with finite groups. Much of my work deals with the values taken by group characters, so my research has a somewhat number-theoretic flavor. I am also interested in the theory of permutation groups and related combinatorial subjects.

  • On a question of Navarro on the field of values of characters of solvable groups, arXiv preprint (2026).

  • A determination of B-groups of order p^4, arXiv preprint (2026).

  • Character Values and Conductors of Low-rank Groups of Lie Type, with N. N. Hung, arXiv preprint (2026).

  • Generalized Characters and Prime Graphs, to appear in the Journal of Group Theory, (2026).

  • Answer to a question of Hung and Tiep on conductors of cyclotomic integers, Arch. Math. (Basel) 126(3) (2026).

  • A block-theoretic proof of Burnside's Normal $p$-complement Theorem, arXiv preprint (2025).

  • On values taken by characters of finite groups, dissertation (2026).
