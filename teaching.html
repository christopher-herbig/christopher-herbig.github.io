Kishwaukee College

While working towards my PhD at Northern Illinois University, I also worked as an adjunct instructor at Kishwaukee College, which is a nearby community college. Most of the courses I taught here were paired with a “support” section that served as a corequisite for students whose placement tests put them just below the threshold to enter the course. These support sections are an extra credit hour and provide the students with extra class time to work on the course material.
